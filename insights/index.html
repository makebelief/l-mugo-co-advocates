LEGAL GUIDE

Before You Sign: Understanding Commercial Agreements

Confirm that the names and capacities of all parties are accurate, and that the agreement clearly sets out what each party is expected to deliver.

Review payment dates, timelines, termination provisions, confidentiality obligations and how disputes will be handled.

Consider whether there are governing-law, compliance or approval requirements that apply to the transaction. If in doubt, ask for advice before signing.

LEGAL GUIDE

What to Check Before Purchasing Property in Kenya

A property transaction involves more than agreeing on a price. Buyers should consider title verification, the seller’s authority and whether the property is subject to restrictions or other claims.

The agreement should be reviewed alongside available searches, completion documents and any required consents or clearances.

The correct process depends on the property and circumstances. Independent legal review can help identify issues early.

LEGAL GUIDE

Employment Contracts: Details That Matter

Employment terms should make the role, remuneration, probation period, leave, benefits and notice arrangements clear.

Workplace policies, confidentiality and disciplinary processes may also be relevant. Employers and employees should be familiar with rights and obligations under Kenyan law.

Where an employment dispute arises, reviewing the relevant documents and correspondence is a useful first step before choosing a response.
